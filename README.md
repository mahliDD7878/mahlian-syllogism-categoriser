this code only deals with categorical syllogisms in order to test the formal soundness/validity.

A - universal affirmation. 'ALL men are mortal'
E - universal negation. 'ALL men are NOT mortal'
I - particular affirmation. 'SOME men are mortal'
O - particular negation. 'SOME men are NOT mortal'

A > E, I, O (A is stronger)
E > I, O
I > O

figure 1: the middle term is used as a subject in the major premise and a predicate in the minor premise
all men are mortal
socrates is a man
socrates is mortal
valid moods: AAA, EAE, AII, EIO

figure 2: the middle term is used as a predicate in both premises:
no mammals are fish
all whales are mammals
no whales are fish
valid moods: EAE, AEE, EIO, AOO

figure 3: the middle term is used as a subject in both premises:
all mammals are warm blooded
all mammals are viviparous animals
some viviparous animals are warm blooded
valid moods: AAI, "AII, IAI, EAO,"OAO, EIO

a conclusion is invalid if the statement in it is stronger than one of the premises
all men are mortal (A)
socrates is a man (I)
all socrates are mortal (I)

no men are mortal
